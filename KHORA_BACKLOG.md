# KHORA_BACKLOG — development ledger

Khora is a brain-structured, LLM-free cognitive architecture in C++20. This is
the working list. Status: PLANNED / IN PROGRESS / SHIPPED / REJECTED / BLOCKED.
Foundational first, always.

## Standing directive

Prioritise CORE ARCHITECTURE and FOUNDATIONAL capability over user-facing
features. Rank: (a) cognitive core, (b) agentic shell (Carapace), (c) quality
machinery (tests, benchmarks, regression detection), only then (d) UI (Vellum).

**And measure before claiming.** The project rule is that nothing is claimed
unless it has been built, run and observed. In the v0.115.0 cycle that rule
killed five mechanisms that looked sound on paper. A benchmark without a dumb
baseline beside it is worth nothing; a result on a self-chosen sample is worth
nothing; a number produced by a harness with a bug in it is worth less than
nothing, because it is believed.

## SHIPPED in v0.115.0

- Four foundational defects fixed: `bundle()` was a union rather than a majority
  vote; `Plexus::ppmi_` normalised by `N^alpha` instead of the partition
  function; the containment cage reported success while running nothing; chains
  encoded with a commutative bind were undirected and traversed at chance.
- Sparse `Sdr` block-code substrate beside the dense `Glyph`, with `SdrUnion`
  and subsampled `Segment` matching.
- `TemporalMemory`: per-cell context, high-order sequences, bursting as a
  novelty signal.
- Category induction validated against WordNet 3.1 as an external answer key.
- Tests for `crucible` and `whetstone`, which had none.

## REJECTED — measured, did not pay, not adopted

Recorded so they are not re-attempted without new evidence.

- **Greedy novelty-seeking as the curiosity drive.** Scores 1.0000
  surprise-remaining at every signal-to-noise ratio tested: it learns nothing and
  spends 92-98% of its attention on unlearnable static. Chasing learning
  *progress* instead also lost to uniform coverage, because a constant-but-noisy
  region manufactures apparent progress out of measurement variance. Fixing that
  properly is a bandit problem — compare progress against its own uncertainty —
  not a tweak. See `bench/curiosity_bench.cpp`.
- **Synaptic pruning.** Measured in two regimes, did not pay, reverted.
- **Redundant encoding as a capability axis.** Bundling R permuted copies into
  one fixed-width vector spends the same bits on the same information. With the
  algebra corrected it is merely harmless; R=1 stays optimal.

## TOP PRIORITY

0. PLANNED: **The new organs are not in the animal.** `Sdr` and `TemporalMemory`
   are built, tested and benchmarked, and `khora_main.cpp` references NEITHER --
   grep returns zero. The running binary is entirely on the dense `Glyph` path
   and still uses `PredictiveColumn` for sequences, which the bench shows scoring
   chance on any sequence that shares a subsequence with another. Everything
   measured in v0.115.0 was measured on a bench, not in the system. Wiring them
   in is the difference between a result and a capability, and it comes first.
   (Synapse has been in exactly this state for 115 releases; do not let these
   two join it.)

1. PLANNED: **Ligature's extracted is-a relations are mostly false.** Measured.
   Everything symbolic — `deduce`, BFS reachability, crystallize — stands on
   them. Either fix extraction (sentence-boundary tokenisation is the obvious
   first move, since the object window currently runs into the next clause) or
   stop treating the output as knowledge.
2. PLANNED: **Decide `synapse`'s fate.** Implemented, tested, and used by
   nothing, while 20+ subsystems are wired by direct reference behind one
   process-wide `std::shared_mutex` that eight background loops contend on.
   Either make it the decoupling it was designed to be, or delete it.
3. PLANNED: **Cortex is not predictive coding.** `PredictiveColumn` is
   exact-context memorisation with no hierarchy, no error signal and no model
   update, growing linearly to a hardcoded 200k cap. `TemporalMemory` now covers
   sequences properly; decide whether `PredictiveColumn` is retired or kept, and
   stop describing it as something it is not.
4. PLANNED: **`cogitator` has no test**, at ~2.5k lines and roughly thirty
   cognitive operations. It is the largest and least verified module in the repo.

## Open questions from measurement

- ~~Khora's graph has hubs but no CORE~~ **CLOSED, and the claim was WRONG.**
  Absolute rich club 0.013 against 0.12-0.98 does read as hubs-but-no-core, but
  every real connectome sits at only 0.72-1.23 times its OWN degree-preserving
  null -- their high absolute figure is mostly degree sequence and density, not
  hubs specially wired to each other. Khora is at 1.39, above all six. Relative
  to what its own degrees predict its hubs are MORE interconnected, not less.
  Same error as the gamma reading one measurement earlier: an absolute figure
  compared across graphs of wildly different density, twice in a row. Imposing
  a core would have been decoration built on a measurement mistake. See
  `bench/richclub_bench.cpp`.
- Also measured there and worth keeping: pruning costs a real amount of absolute
  rich club (0.072 unpruned to 0.023 at the shipped cap of 160), but the
  RANKING is not the cause -- ppmi*log(1+c) and raw co-occurrence give 0.0231
  and 0.0239, indistinguishable. The cap does the work, and across caps from 40
  to unpruned the vs-null ratio stays in a narrow 1.12-1.35 band. Absolute rich
  club is a density readout, not a structural property.

## Cognitive core

5. PLANNED: Soma — the "homeostatic weight adaptation" it documents does not
   exist; setpoints and decay rates are constants fixed at construction. Add
   outcome-driven adaptation, bounds-check the Drive index (`Drive::_Count`
   indexes out of bounds today), and persist the nexus — personality currently
   resets on every restart.
6. PLANNED: Reverie — the satisfaction gate was a no-op at shipped defaults, so
   100% of dreams were retained forever into an uncapped lattice Ballast does not
   know about. Re-measure now that `bundle()` is fixed, since the cause was
   bundle-of-2 behaving as OR.
7. PLANNED: Lattice persistence tiering — hot/cold split with atomic flush.
8. PLANNED: `Lattice::query` is a brute-force linear scan, non-deterministic in
   ties, and fully sorts before truncating to k. It is the system's core recall
   primitive and the measured bottleneck.
